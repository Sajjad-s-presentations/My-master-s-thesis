# B Zar font files

B Zar and B Zar Bold are the original Borna Rayaneh font family requested for
this thesis (not XB Zar or a substituted font). Retrieved from the public
BFonts archive maintained by fzerorubigd:

https://github.com/fzerorubigd/persian-fonts-linux/tree/gh-pages

Archive: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fzerorubigd/persian-fonts-linux/gh-pages/bfonts.zip

The fonts retain their embedded copyright and naming metadata.
